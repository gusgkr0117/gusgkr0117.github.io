# Complex Line Bundle on a Torus

Let
$$
E=\mathbb{C}/(\mathbb{Z}+\mathbb{Z}\tau)
$$
be a complex torus.

A complex line bundle can be described by identifying fibers over lattice-equivalent points.

For example, consider the identification
$$
(z,r)\sim (z+\tau, e^{-\pi i\tau-2\pi i z}r).
$$

The scalar
$$
e^{-\pi i\tau-2\pi i z}
$$
is a transition factor: it tells us how the fiber coordinate $r$ changes when we move by the period $\tau$.

In the picture, the phase of this transition factor is shown as color on the torus.
