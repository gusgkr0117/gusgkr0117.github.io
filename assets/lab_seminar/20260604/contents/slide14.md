# Very Ample Line Bundle Example

Fix a complex torus
$$
E=\mathbb{C}/(\mathbb{Z}+\mathbb{Z}\tau)
$$
and a very ample line bundle of degree $3$.

This line bundle has three independent holomorphic sections:
$$
s_0,\quad s_1,\quad s_2.
$$

Explicitly, we can take the degree $3$ theta basis
$$
s_k(z)=\sum_{n\in\mathbb{Z}}
\exp\left(3\pi i\left(n+\frac{k}{3}\right)^2\tau
+6\pi i\left(n+\frac{k}{3}\right)z\right),
\qquad k=0,1,2.
$$

Choosing a point $z\in E$ determines three section values
$$
s_0(z),\quad s_1(z),\quad s_2(z).
$$

These values become projective coordinates:
$$
z \mapsto [s_0(z):s_1(z):s_2(z)]\in\mathbb{P}^2.
$$
