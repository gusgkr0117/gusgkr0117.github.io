# Zero Sum of a Theta Section

Choose a linear combination of the three degree $3$ theta basis sections:
$$
F(z)=a_0s_0(z)+a_1s_1(z)+a_2s_2(z).
$$

For a generic choice of $a_0,a_1,a_2$, this section has three zeros:
$$
z_1,\quad z_2,\quad z_3.
$$

The theta transformation rule fixes their sum on the torus.
After normalizing the origin used by the embedding, this becomes
$$
z_1+z_2+z_3=0
\quad \text{in } \mathbb C/\Lambda.
$$

Changing $a_0,a_1,a_2$ changes the line in projective space.
The three zeros move, but their normalized sum stays zero.
Without that normalization, their raw sum is a fixed point of $\mathbb C/\Lambda$.
