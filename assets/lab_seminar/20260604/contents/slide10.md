# Definition of Line Bundle

Definition. A line bundle over a space $X$ is a map
$$
\pi:L\to X
$$
such that each fiber
$$
L_x=\pi^{-1}(x)
$$
is a one-dimensional vector space, and locally $L$ looks like
$$
U\times \mathbb{R}
$$
or
$$
U\times \mathbb{C}.
$$

The key point is local triviality:
locally it looks like a product, but globally it may be twisted.

Example. The Möbius strip is a real line bundle over the circle $S^1$.

Each point of $S^1$ has a line attached to it, but after going once around the circle, the line returns with a twist.
